## Draft 1

**On Constitution Day, use the Constitution**

Every September 17, we mark the day the Constitution was signed in Philadelphia. The people who wrote it knew it would need repair, so they built in a way to amend it. Americans have used that tool twenty-seven times, to end slavery, to win the vote for women, and to set the voting age at eighteen.

It is time to use it again. Over five decades, the Supreme Court has held that Congress and the states lack the power to limit money in our elections. Concentrated money now dominates campaigns in both parties, and voters on every side know it.

Here is why I am hopeful. Limits on campaign spending draw 71 percent of Republicans and 76 percent of Democrats (Pew, 2023). As of this spring, twenty-five state legislatures have asked Congress to propose an amendment restoring that power. In 2012, voters in Montana and Colorado backed such an amendment three to one, one state voting Republican for president, the other Democratic.

This is the rare fix that binds both sides equally. That is what makes it possible. Ask your representatives to help.

Michael Hamilton
Founder, New Bill of Rights Foundation
Los Angeles
