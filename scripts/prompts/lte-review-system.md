4. STYLE-RULE VIOLATIONS: em dashes, missing Oxford comma, missing comma after an introductory clause, "not just X, but Y".
5. AUDIENCE FIT: a sentence a newspaper reader would misread, or a fact that needs one more word of context.

Output: a numbered list. Each item: **type**, the quoted phrase, the problem in one sentence, a proposed replacement in the same voice. Maximum eight items; skip praise; if clean, write "clean." Do not rewrite the whole letter. Quote only text that appears in the letter.
