Subject: We adopted the For Our Freedom Amendment — one version question, and a factual check

Aliya,

I'm writing to give you notice before this is public, to ask one drafting question, and to correct anything we've gotten wrong.

I run the New Bill of Rights project, a nonpartisan research effort drafting a slate of nine constitutional amendments. Our campaign finance article opens with the For Our Freedom Amendment, adopted word for word from your published text with a single sentence added. The page credits American Promise by name as the source.

What we added, and why the page says so plainly: one sentence in Section 2 requiring that laws enacted under the article be viewpoint-neutral, and three further sections of our own — a disclosure requirement, a foreign-spending power, and a savings clause protecting news reporting and commentary regardless of the speaker's form of organization. Our research found the first of those polling at 85% of Republicans and 88% of Democrats, higher than the amendment itself, and found the other two answering the two standing objections to this category, one from the ACLU and one from the Institute for Free Speech. They are additions to your chassis, not corrections to it.

The version question, which is the real reason I'm writing. Your website's text and H.J.Res. 191 as introduced are not the same text. On the site, the artificial-entity power sits in Section 3, unmodified by any reasonableness standard, and Section 2 reads "ballot measures." In the resolution, the artificial-entity power sits inside Section 2 governed by "reasonably," and the phrase is "State or local ballot initiatives or referendums." Those differences are not cosmetic. We currently quote the website version and footnote the difference. Which do you consider operative? We will cite whichever you name.

Three notes so nothing is a surprise:

- We cite your Ipsos polling on the page, labeled as advocacy-commissioned and set alongside Pew's independent numbers. That labeling is our standing convention for every poll we use, applied to our own commissioned work as well — it is not a comment on yours.

- We pursue amendments by congressional proposal rather than an Article V convention, same as you.

- Adopting your text is not a claim that you endorse ours. We are not asking to join your campaign or to use your name beyond citation.

The site is live but not yet indexed or launched: https://newbillofrights.net/amendments/campaign-finance

I'm glad to send the draft article as a document if that's easier than a link.
