## The premise of both emails

We adopted each organization's amendment text into our own draft. That is a fact they will
eventually discover, and it is better delivered by us than found. So neither email is a pitch.
Each one does three things: gives notice, states plainly what we changed and why, and asks them
to correct anything we got wrong before the site launches.

The standing rule from the org pass — **adopt-their-text ≠ join-their-campaign** — is stated
explicitly in both emails. We are not asking for endorsement, coalition membership, or use of
their name beyond citation. That non-ask is what makes the email safe to send before the
foundation exists.

### Reply received 2026-08-24 — Aaron Dukette

**Both accuracy questions answered, and both confirm what the page already says.**

1. **Fourteen states**, single-subject Article V convention call — "the last of these being Arizona
   on April 21st, 2026." Independently corroborated: the Arizona Senate passed **HCR2043** on
   April 21, 2026, **16–13** ([USTL release](https://termlimits.com/victory-arizona-legislature-turns-up-heat-on-congress-by-passing-term-limits-resolution/);
   [LegiScan roll call](https://legiscan.com/AZ/rollcall/HCR2043/id/1686579), 403 to plain fetch —
   Firecrawl if the roll-call detail is needed). ⚠️ **USTL's own release does not call Arizona the
   fourteenth** — it says only "8 states that we have passed in the last two years." The number
   fourteen is Dukette's, named and dated, which is *better* attribution than the progress page but
   is still the organization's own count. Keep attributing it.
2. **H.J.Res. 12 / S.J.Res. 1 (119th) confirmed current.** The page's citation stands.

**⚠️ He misread what we did.** His words: *"Glad to hear you've endorsed our resolution!"* We
adopted their text; we did not endorse their resolution. The phrase is ambiguous between two
readings and **both are wrong in ways that matter**:

- If "our resolution" means the **Article V convention call**, it collides directly with the
  founder's recorded congressional-proposal decision and would code us into the convention camp —
  the exact exposure [CROSS_CUTTING_ORGS.md](CROSS_CUTTING_ORGS.md) flags, given the ~230-org
  anti-convention coalition.
- If it means **H.J.Res. 12 as a standalone vehicle**, it still implies we back their bill rather
  than having borrowed its language into a nine-amendment slate that continues into judicial
  provisions they hold no position on.

Corrected in `email-ustl-reply.txt` — warmly, without criticizing their route, and with an offer to
send them our exact credit language for their sign-off before launch. **This is the item to watch:**
if USTL repeats "New Bill of Rights endorsed our resolution" in public materials, unwinding it later
is far more costly than the one paragraph it takes to fix now.

**Also:** he attached a document on the reasoning behind the 3/2 figures. If it arrived, it belongs
in `research/cache/` and is a genuine source for the term-limits page's stated trade-off, where we
currently give the twelve-year evidence and our reasons for taking their numbers anyway.

  numbers, which is what removes the "and no longer limit" collision the memo flagged. Nothing to
  disclose. Do not raise it; raising a resolved conflict manufactures one.
- **Any request for endorsement, meeting, funding, or coalition membership.** The first email
  earns a reply by being useful to them. An ask converts it into a solicitation and lowers the
  reply rate.
