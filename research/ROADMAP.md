# Roadmap

1. **List the possible amendments** — collect every candidate idea with attribution. → See [AMENDMENT_IDEAS.md](AMENDMENT_IDEAS.md)
2. **Abstract and categorize the focus areas** — group ideas into themes that can be tested against the mission's full-spectrum standard. → See [CATEGORIES.md](CATEGORIES.md) (target and rejected categories)
3. **Build a survey** — start digging in with people across the political spectrum to find where real consensus exists. Existing public polling to build on: [POLLING_RESEARCH.md](POLLING_RESEARCH.md)
4. **Set up a monthly salon in Los Angeles** — recurring in-person discussion to develop and pressure-test proposals. Topic queue: [SALON_TOPICS.md](SALON_TOPICS.md). Outreach plan: [SALON_OUTREACH_PLAN.md](SALON_OUTREACH_PLAN.md); target lists: [SALON_OUTREACH_TARGETS.md](SALON_OUTREACH_TARGETS.md)
