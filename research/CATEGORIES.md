# Categories

The focus areas the foundation is targeting, and the ones deliberately rejected. Every target category eventually has to pass the mission's test: appeal across the full spectrum from MAGA Republicans to socialist Democrats, and address a problem that threatens the future of the country.

## Rejected Categories

Topics deliberately excluded from the platform. These aren't judgments that the issues don't matter — they're judgments that a category cannot pass the full-spectrum test and would sink the broader effort by coding the whole project as partisan.

- **Abortion** *(rejected 2026-08-06)* — Deeply polarized moral issue with no cross-spectrum consensus position; including it in any form would immediately code the platform as partisan.
- **Voting Rights** *(rejected 2026-08-06)* — Currently coded as a partisan battleground (access-vs-integrity framing splits cleanly along party lines). Suggested by Ryan Ward as a target area, but fails the bipartisan test for now.
- **Electoral College & Presidential Elections** *(rejected 2026-08-06)* — Fails the bipartisan test. Open question: what is the real issue here other than perceived unfairness from the left?
- **Supreme Court Expansion** *(rejected 2026-08-06)* — Not bipartisan enough. (Judicial term limits and ethics rules remain a target — see Target Category 2.)
- **Senate / Congressional Structure** *(rejected 2026-08-06)* — Not bipartisan enough.
- **Election Technology & Modernization** *(rejected 2026-08-06)* — Not bipartisan and not popular.
- **Civic Information & Informed Citizenry** *(rejected 2026-08-06)* — Great idea for a new company or non-profit, but doesn't yet meet the bar for popular support. Adding good-government ideas is interesting, but unclear how to build enough popular support to pass an amendment.
- **Eliminate Qualified Immunity for Policing** *(rejected 2026-08-06)* — Weakest bipartisan case of the original targets: Republican support for elimination is only 39–46% in every credible poll, the data is stale (2020–21, post-George Floyd peak), and support was already declining. The one framing that works — "hold officers accountable for misconduct" — polls at 84% (64% R). Could return as a target if reframed around accountability and validated with fresh polling.
- **Balanced Budget** *(rejected 2026-08-06)* — Strong but shallow: 69–80% support in the abstract, but the only trade-off experiment ever run (Fox 2011) shows support collapsing to 31% when entitlement cuts are specified. Democrats defect on benefit cuts, Republicans on tax increases.
- **Statehood for DC and Puerto Rico** *(rejected 2026-08-18, from the candidate list)* — DC: 13% R (Yahoo/YouGov 2021), 15% R and only 39% D (Gallup 2019), two House passages with no Republican yeas — fails the bipartisan floor and the symmetry test (admission is a one-time act with a known partisan result). Puerto Rico: 41–45% R, Weak, and admission is a statute under Article IV §3 — fails why-an-amendment; the statutory route stays on the record. Path back for DC: "representation without statehood," never polled. Memo: [rejected/STATEHOOD_REJECTED.md](rejected/STATEHOOD_REJECTED.md).
- **Filibuster reform** *(rejected 2026-08-18, from the candidate list)* — 8% R for elimination and 19% D for keeping it as is (Monmouth 2022); both directions fail; the two 48–52 votes on the decision of the chair (2013, 2017) with the parties exchanged are the cleanest regime-coding case in the corpus — fails the symmetry test. Path back: a forward-looking poll of the "supermajority to kill" inversion clearing 45% in both parties across two Senate majorities. Memo: [rejected/FILIBUSTER_REFORM_REJECTED.md](rejected/FILIBUSTER_REFORM_REJECTED.md).
- **Expanding the House of Representatives** *(rejected 2026-08-18, from the candidate list)* — 23% R / 35% D (Pew 2023) and 11% R when costs are named (2006); the size of the House is a statute since 1929, so why-an-amendment is weak. Symmetric by simulation. Path back: an independent poll of the Wyoming-Rule / cube-root framing clearing 45% R after a statutory enlargement has been tried. Memo: [rejected/HOUSE_EXPANSION_REJECTED.md](rejected/HOUSE_EXPANSION_REJECTED.md).
