## Thesis

Constitutional amendments every American can agree on.

## Pillars

Freedom · Democracy · Rule of Law · Good Government

## Full-spectrum test

Every proposal must clear a support floor in *both* parties — from MAGA Republicans to socialist Democrats — before it makes the list. If it can't, it doesn't matter how good the idea is: it's out.

## We show our work

We've rejected {rejectedCount} categories that couldn't pass the full-spectrum bar — not because the issues don't matter, but because they can't yet clear it. Transparency about what didn't make the list is the point.

## The founding fathers made it difficult for a reason

Amending the Constitution takes two-thirds of Congress and three-quarters of the states. That high bar is why we only advance proposals with genuine support across the whole spectrum — anything less can't ratify, and shouldn't.
